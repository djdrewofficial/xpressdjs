Choosing a DJ is one of the most important decisions you'll make for your wedding. The right one elevates the mood, energizes your guests, and keeps the whole day flowing. Here's what to look for.

## 1. Wedding experience specifically

A club DJ and a wedding DJ are not the same job. Weddings need someone who can MC introductions, manage a timeline, coordinate with your other vendors, and handle the emotional moments with care. Ask how many *weddings* they do a year.

## 2. Can they read a room?

The single biggest difference between an average DJ and a great one is crowd-reading. Ask how they decide what to play next. The right answer is about *your guests*, not their favorite tracks.

## 3. Bilingual & multicultural range

If your guest list spans cultures and generations, you want a DJ who can speak — and spin — to all of them. For South Florida weddings, bilingual hosting is often a must.

## 4. A real planning process

Great DJs don't wing it. We plan every song and moment with you in the **Vibo app**, so your must-plays, do-not-plays, and timeline are locked in long before the big day.

## 5. Reviews and consistency

Look for a long track record of five-star reviews — and a team that has never missed an event.
