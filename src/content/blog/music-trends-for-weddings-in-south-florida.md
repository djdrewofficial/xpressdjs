South Florida weddings have a vibe all their own — tropical energy, multicultural families, and crowds that genuinely love to dance. Here's what's keeping local dance floors packed right now.

## Latin-inspired everything

Reggaeton, salsa, and merengue aren't "special requests" here — they're the heartbeat of a South Florida reception. The best moments come from weaving them seamlessly with Top 40 so the whole room moves together.

## Bilingual hosting

Couples increasingly want an MC who can host in **both English and Spanish** — keeping every generation informed, included, and on the floor. It's one of the most-requested things we do.

## Live remixes & open format

Guests love hearing a familiar song reimagined live. Open-format DJs who can blend genres on the fly — and read the crowd in real time — create a night that feels custom, not canned.

## Cinematic moments

Cold sparks on the grand entrance. Dancing on the clouds for the first dance. These visual "wow" moments are trending hard because they make the music *feel* even bigger — and they make incredible content.
