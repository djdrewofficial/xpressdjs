Creating a seamless wedding day timeline is essential for keeping your event running smoothly. Here's how to plan your timeline with your DJ to ensure that every key moment flows effortlessly.

## 1. Pre-Ceremony Music

Your DJ should start playing music as guests arrive for the ceremony. Choose soft, welcoming songs that set the tone without overpowering conversations.

## 2. Ceremony Music

From the processional to the recessional, make sure your DJ knows exactly when each song should start and stop. Timing is key to making the ceremony feel smooth and emotional.

## 3. Cocktail Hour

During cocktail hour, the music should be upbeat but not overpowering. Your DJ can help set a relaxed, celebratory mood as guests mingle and enjoy drinks.

## 4. Dinner and Speeches

Coordinate with your DJ to keep the music low during dinner and speeches so that conversations and toasts can be heard. After the formalities, gradually increase the energy as the dance floor opens.

## 5. Grand Exit

For the grand exit, work with your DJ to play a special song that signals the end of the night. This could be an emotional ballad or a high-energy anthem, depending on the vibe you want to end with.

## Conclusion

- Planning your wedding day timeline with your DJ ensures that every moment is perfectly timed, from the first song to the last. A well-coordinated schedule will keep your guests entertained and ensure that your wedding flows smoothly.
