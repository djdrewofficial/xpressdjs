If you're planning a wedding in South Florida, the DJ is one of the first vendors you'll want to price out. A lot of companies won't post numbers online. We do, so here is what our DJs, extras and photo booths cost at Xpress Entertainment, and what changes the final price. You can also see everything on one page at [our pricing page](/pricing/).

## What a wedding DJ costs at Xpress

Each DJ on our team sets their own starting rate based on experience and demand. Starting rates cover your cocktail hour and reception, and new bookings include our standard dance floor lighting.

- **[DJ Drew Segura](/dj-drew-segura/)**, starting at $2,750. Owner and lead DJ. A bilingual English and Spanish DJ and MC who loves a multicultural crowd.
- **[DJ Atomic](/djatomic/)**, starting at $2,500. Originally from Saint Lucia, known for multicultural sets and clean mixing.
- **[DJ Phil](/djphil/)**, starting at $1,950. Open format DJ and MC who speaks English, Spanish and Portuguese.
- **[DJ ReRe](/djrere/)**, starting at $1,950. DJ and MC who builds the soundtrack around your crowd.
- **[DJ Naqeeb](/djnaqeeb/)**, starting at $1,950. Caribbean and West Indian sets. He DJs only, so you bring your own MC or add one from our team.

## What changes your price

No two weddings we play are built the same way. Your price comes down to the DJ you pick, whether your ceremony needs its own sound, and which extras you add. Everything goes into one booking agreement, so you see your total before you lock in your date.

Ceremony coverage is $250. That covers 30 minutes of music before the ceremony, the ceremony itself, a microphone for your officiant, and sound so every guest hears the vows.

## Lighting, effects and live music

- **[Uplighting](/epic-extras-1/lighting/)**, $450. Color matched light around the walls, columns or the spots you care about. We don't price it by the number of lights.
- **[Monogram projection](/epic-extras-1/lighting/)**, $375. Your names, initials, date or logo.
- **[Club style light show](/epic-extras-1/lighting/)**, $750. Moving lights sized to your room.
- **[Cold sparks](/epic-extras-1/cold-sparks/)**, $450. Two machines for your entrance, first dance or cake cutting. Your venue has to approve them.
- **[Dancing on the Clouds](/epic-extras-1/dancing-on-the-clouds/)**, $450. One use, usually during your first dance.
- **[CO2 gun or cannon](/epic-extras-1/)**, $375. One gun or cannon with one tank.
- **[Laser experience](/epic-extras-1/)**, $750. Two lasers set about 10 feet up, timed to the night.
- **Hora Loca**, $1,650. A 45 minute show with two party animators, an LED stilts robot dancer and a photo session at the end.
- **Cocktail hour violinist**, $575. One hour of live violin while your guests mingle.

## Wedding DJ and photo booth packages in South Florida

You can add any of our photo booths to your DJ in the same booking agreement. For example, DJ Phil with ceremony coverage and the Lux Photo-Booth starts at $3,075.

- **[Lux Photo-Booth](/south-florida-photo-booth-rental/)**, $875 for 4 hours. Canon DSLR, instant prints, sharing to guests' phones and an attendant.
- **[Glam Black & White Booth](/glam-booth/)**, $1,200 for 4 hours. Black and white portraits with instant 4x6 prints and your monogram.
- **[Roaming Booth](/south-florida-photo-booth-rental/)**, $750 for 4 hours, or $350 for cocktail hour. An attendant walks around and guests get photos by text or email.
- **[Keychain Booth](/keychain-photo-booth-station/)**, from $1,500 for 4 hours. The Lux experience plus 50 custom keychains.
- **AI Photo Booth**, from $1,500 for 4 hours. Guest photos turned into themed images.
